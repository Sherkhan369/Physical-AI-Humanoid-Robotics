# Welcome to the Physical AI Textbook

This textbook provides a comprehensive introduction to Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics, bridging the gap between digital intelligence and embodied systems. It covers foundational concepts in ROS 2, digital twin simulations with Gazebo and Unity, advanced AI for robotics using NVIDIA Isaac™, and cutting-edge Vision-Language-Action (VLA) integration.

## Target Audience

This book is designed for university students and researchers at an intermediate to advanced level, assuming proficiency in Python and basic knowledge of AI/Machine Learning concepts. It's suitable for courses in robotics, AI, computer vision, and autonomous systems.

## What You Will Learn

Each module is carefully structured to build your knowledge incrementally:

-   **Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)**: Master the fundamentals and advanced communication paradigms of ROS 2 for robotic control and navigation.
-   **Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)**: Learn to create high-fidelity robotic simulations, from basic models in Gazebo to complex humanoid interactions in Unity.
-   **Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)**: Dive into GPU-accelerated simulation, perception, and manipulation using NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac ROS, including reinforcement learning with Isaac Gym.
-   **Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)**: Explore the integration of computer vision, natural language understanding, and robotic action for intuitive human-robot interaction and embodied AI systems.

## How to Use This Textbook

This textbook is designed for hands-on learning. Each chapter includes:

-   Clear **Learning Objectives**.
-   In-depth **Core Theory** with mathematical equations (KaTeX).
-   **Runnable Code Examples** in Python (and C# for Unity) to illustrate concepts.
-   **Diagrams** (Mermaid, SVG, PNG/JPG) for visual understanding.
-   **Hands-on Exercises** with expected outputs to apply your knowledge.
-   **Summary & Further Reading** with IEEE citations for deeper exploration.

We encourage you to actively engage with the code examples and exercises to gain practical experience. The `Installation Guide` in the appendices provides detailed instructions for setting up your development environment.

Happy learning!
