# About the Authors

This textbook was collaboratively developed by a diverse team of experts in AI, robotics, and education, with a shared passion for advancing knowledge in physical AI.

## Our Vision

We believe that the future of AI lies in its physical embodiment—systems that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world. Our goal is to empower the next generation of innovators with the tools and understanding to build these intelligent physical systems, pushing the boundaries of what is possible.
